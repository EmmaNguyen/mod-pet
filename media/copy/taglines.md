## About 150 words

Mochi is a pixel-art companion for Claude Code on macOS. She floats above your message box and keeps you informed about every chat without you having to watch them. Her pose, badge, and colours show whether a chat is working, needs your OK, is ready, or is blocked.

When a chat asks for permission, her card has Allow and Deny buttons, so you can answer without switching windows. Clicking her lists your recent chats, and a label above her shows your usage limits.

She comes in six skins. Mochi Studio, a separate app, changes her colour, eyes, accessory, and name.

Mochi is a Claude Code plugin with two macOS apps. It makes no network requests of its own. It is open source under MIT, and it is a work in progress. Install is a source build for macOS 13 or later.
